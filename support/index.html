Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Version 1.0 can be used without creating an account.

Are my screenshots public?
No. Screenshots are used only to provide the read you request. The app does not post conversations publicly.

Can I delete my data?
Yes. Open Settings → Data controls to delete Rooms, export your data, or erase all locally stored Read the Room data.

Why did the app read the speakers backwards?
Before running a read, confirm whether your own messages are on the Right or Left side of the screenshot.

What if a read seems wrong?
AI analysis can be incomplete or incorrect. Use the Receipt and Reality Check sections to compare the result with the actual conversation.
