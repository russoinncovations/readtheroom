READ THE ROOM

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Plain-language summary: Read the Room is designed for private conversation analysis. Your Rooms and extracted messages are stored on your device. When you request an AI-powered feature, the content needed for that request is transmitted securely to an AI processing service. We do not sell conversation data, use it for advertising, or post it publicly.

Information you choose to provide

Read the Room may process conversation screenshots, message text, names visible in screenshots, drafts you type, Room names, and questions you ask. The app does not require an account in version 1.0.

On-device storage

Rooms, extracted messages, saved reads, settings, and screenshots you choose to keep are stored on your device. Local app files use iOS file protection. By default, original screenshots are not retained by the app after processing unless you choose to keep them as evidence.

AI processing

When you request a Quick Read, Room analysis, Ask the Room, or Check My Response, the content needed for that request is transmitted over an encrypted connection for AI processing. Read the Room's AI features are hosted through Rork's AI infrastructure. The current production configuration routes requests to Google Gemini, with Anthropic Claude configured as a fallback. Depending on availability and routing, Rork and the selected model provider process the submitted content to return the result you requested.

Read the Room does not operate a separate server-side conversation database. Infrastructure and model providers may retain service logs or request data according to their own security, abuse-prevention, diagnostic, and retention practices. Read the Room does not use conversation content for advertising and does not sell it.

What is not public

Your conversations are not posted to a public feed and are not shared with other Read the Room users. Sharing a result is initiated by you.

Your controls

Inside the app you can delete individual Rooms, delete stored screenshots, export locally stored app data, or erase all locally stored Read the Room data.

Tracking and advertising

Read the Room does not use conversation content for targeted advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Children

Read the Room is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit another person's private information unless you are authorized to do so.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to evolvebtc@gmail.com.
